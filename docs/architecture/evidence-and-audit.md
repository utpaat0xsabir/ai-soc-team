# Evidence, lineage and audit

Evidence records carry tenant and source context plus content integrity metadata. Evidence lineage links derived records to their inputs using tenant-qualified endpoints. The API supports create, read and integrity verification. A semantic claim needs a valid source and a traceable observation; a content digest alone establishes neither factual truth nor authorized source identity. [Models](../../services/api/app/database/models.py) · [Evidence routes](../../services/api/app/api/v1/evidence.py) · [Hashing](../../services/api/app/evidence/hashing.py)

Treat observations, interpretations, hypotheses and conclusions as distinct types. At the analyst boundary verify classification, provenance, tenant, integrity and approved disclosure before making a bounded snapshot. The stored evidence and PostgreSQL source/classification adapter work has distinct review and release status. [Current state](current-state.md)

The existing audit design uses a per-tenant sequence, previous hash and record hash. The writer serializes same-tenant appends and couples evidence and audit within the relevant transaction. Database triggers defend append-only history against ordinary mutation. Verify chain continuity and content through the integrity endpoint. Owners and privileged administrators remain trust boundaries. External anchoring, independent custody, and disaster recovery are separate deployment controls. [Audit integrity specification](../security/audit-log-integrity.md)

For future artifacts, store exact source/trust decision, classification, stable identifiers, hash and transformation version. Preserve original evidence separately from normalized records, tool observations, claims and action outcomes. Record who accessed or exported sensitive evidence. For retention, define tenant policy, legal hold, backup retention, and an auditable deletion protocol compatible with preserved integrity proofs. Keep raw content and credentials out of operational logs.
